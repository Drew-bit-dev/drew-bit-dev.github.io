This project saw me critically examining their current process and transforming it into a cleaner and faster-running product through the combination of upwards of 30 automated business rule checks into a single Salesforce Flow. The change improved agent work time by allowing for records to be saved and updated significantly faster, while leaving a modular product that could easily be updated in the future as business rules were added or removed.
